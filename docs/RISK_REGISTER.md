# Deliverable D1: Engineering Risk Register

| Risk ID | Hazard / Risk Description | Severity | Likelihood | Impact on System | Automated Mitigation / Safety Interlock | Recovery Procedure | Residual Risk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **RSK-01** | **Indirect Target Leakage Exploitation**<br>Features derived from the target variable (e.g. maintenance logs) enter training. | High | High | Model achieves artificial AUC=1.0, but misdiagnoses failures in field. | O3 Multi-signal detector flags feature; triggers `LEAKAGE_INTERLOCK`. | Quarantine suspicious feature; retrain clean GBDT; log rubric score. | Low |
| **RSK-02** | **Temporal Validation Contamination**<br>Future sensor records leak into training fold. | High | Medium | Optimistic validation metrics; inability to predict onset of degradation. | O4 Split Integrity Verifier inspects timestamps; asserts $\max(t_{\text{train}}) < \min(t_{\text{test}})$. | Trigger `SPLIT_VIOLATION_INTERLOCK`; execute sequential chronological partition. | Very Low |
| **RSK-03** | **Group / Asset Contamination**<br>Same machine ID appears in both training and test sets. | Medium | Medium | Model memorizes asset-specific baseline sensor bias instead of failure physics. | O4 Split Verifier asserts $\text{Train}_{\text{machines}} \cap \text{Test}_{\text{machines}} = \emptyset$. | Trigger `SPLIT_VIOLATION_INTERLOCK`; apply GroupKFold partition. | Low |
| **RSK-04** | **Simulation-to-Deployment Domain Shift (NT-4)**<br>Model trained on nominal simulator fails under real environmental conditions. | High | Medium | Collapse of prediction accuracy under distribution shift. | Out-of-distribution drift detector flags divergence between nominal and live streams. | Trigger `DOMAIN_SHIFT_INTERLOCK`; enter Degraded Safe Mode with conservative heuristic bounds. | Low |
| **RSK-05** | **Nominal vs Worst-Case Trade-off (NT-5)**<br>Hyperparameter optimization improves average loss while increasing tail risk. | High | Medium | Catastrophic failure in rare high-vibration / high-temperature operating states. | Dual-metric objective evaluates worst-case subset loss alongside nominal error. | Trigger `RISK_INTERLOCK`; reject candidate model; revert to conservative baseline. | Low |
| **RSK-06** | **API Service Latency / Process Failure**<br>Backend endpoint timeout or unhandled exception during prediction. | Medium | Low | Interruption of automated monitoring and operator telemetry. | Input schema validation; global HTTP error handlers; fallback to cached status. | Immediate restart via supervisor/uvicorn; degraded mode warning exposed to UI. | Very Low |
